Welcome To Lesotho Tourism:

Lesotho is a beautiful mountain kingdom in Southern Africa,
Also the only country on Earth that's entirely above 3,000 feet.
It's known as Africa's "KINDOM IN THE SKY" Completely surrounded by South Africa.

It is known for its highlands, traditional Basotho culture,Waterfalls and friendly communities.


Why visit Lesotho?

  • Beautiful mountain scenery
  • Traditional Basotho culture
  • Horse riding and hiking
  • Peaceful natural attractions
